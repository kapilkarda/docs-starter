## Adding a CallHippo Dialer

CallHippo is a cloud telephony provider that supplies virtual calling numbers. Once connected, your assistant can use the CallHippo number to place outbound calls to your customers through your campaigns. When you create a CallHippo dialer you only need to provide three details:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | A friendly label to identify this dialer in your account (e.g. `Sales Line`). |
| **Auth Token** | Your CallHippo API key / access token, used to authenticate outbound calls. |
| **Contact Number** | The CallHippo virtual number your customers will be called from. |

<Note>
  Unlike Twilio or SIP, a CallHippo dialer does <span style={{ fontWeight: 700 }}>not</span> require a SID or a SIP termination URI — only the Name, Auth Token, and Contact Number.
</Note>

<span style={{ color: "#1e40af" }}>
  Follow these steps to add your CallHippo Dialer
</span>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Auth Token from CallHippo">
    1. Log in to your [CallHippo dashboard](https://app.callhippo.com).
    2. From the sidebar, open **Integrations**.
    3. Click **Integrate** (or **API**) and copy your **API Key / Access Token** — this is your Auth Token.

    <Note>Keep your Auth Token secure — it grants API access to your CallHippo account.</Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get your Contact Number">
    Purchase or select the virtual number you want your customers to be called from in CallHippo's **Numbers** section. This number is your Contact Number.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the Dialer in VocbotAI">
    1. In VocbotAI, go to **Call Platforms → Dialers**.
    2. Click **Add Dialer** and choose **CallHippo** as the dialer type.
    3. Enter your **Name**, **Auth Token**, and **Contact Number**.
    4. Click **Save** to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<span style={{ fontWeight: 700 }}>Congratulations</span> 🔥🎉 You have successfully
added your CallHippo dialer 😎
